Comprehensive Solution for the Utilization of Mud Deposits from Klaipėda Port

Project Status: Ready for Implementation

Relevance

Due to years of dredging operations in the Klaipėda Port area, over 300 thousand tons of mud deposits have accumulated, taking up significant land areas, posing an environmental threat to the region through toxicity and uncontrolled emissions, and also containing valuable components, including silicon dioxide (SiO₂).

Processing Technology Scheme

The basis is low-temperature physicochemical processes implemented on high-productivity equipment:

1. Mud Activation (Stage 1)

— Disintegration of mud structure using vortex layer devices and ABC-150 type activators
— Purpose: obtaining aqueous pulps (sludge, slime).

2. Three-Phase Separation (Stage 2)

— SUC-3C installation (Special Three-Phase Separation Centrifuge) provides separation of: water, petroleum products, solid fractions (sand).

3. Drying (Stage 3)

— Highly efficient drying in a vibroboiling layer, energy-saving even with high mud moisture.

4. Silicon Dioxide Extraction (Stage 4)

— Chemical treatment for silicon dioxide extraction in the form of microsilica
— Installation: "sublimator-desublimator" for obtaining high-purity amorphous SiO₂.

5. Organic Processing (Stage 5)

— Universal pyrolysis furnace "Potochok", which provides utilization of toxic organic residues, heat and electricity generation, and production of commercial organics.

6. Recirculating Water Treatment (Stage 6)

— Final filtration for water reuse in the process.

Expected Results

— Environmental cleanup of the region and soil reclamation
— Elimination of toxic emissions and reduction of carbon footprint
— Production of commercial synthetic silica (SiO₂) with a yield of 92-97%
— Significant reduction in operational and energy costs
— Utilization of accompanying toxic substances.

Land Reclamation

The official representative office of the International Information Nobel Center in Ukraine owns technologies for:

— Comprehensive reclamation of lands after mud removal
— Restoration of ecosystems on contaminated territories.

Conclusion

The proposed solution allows for:

— Effectively utilize accumulated mud deposits
— Transform environmental threat into a resource
— Implement an innovative approach to closed-loop mud processing with valuable product output.

We are ready to implement the project in partnership with government bodies, industrial and environmental organizations.

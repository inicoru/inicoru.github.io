Serhii Ivanovych Sokolovskyi

Director

Vice-Rector, Professor of the Department of Fundamental Disciplines of European Medical University

Academician of the Academy of Technological Sciences of Ukraine and Academy of Political and Legal Sciences of Ukraine. Honorary Professor of the Academy of Holistic Medicine (Falkensee, Germany). Advisor to the Director of the National Coordination Center for Anti-Corruption. Chairman of the Honorary Board of the Charitable Foundation "Volunteers of the World", Director of the Charitable Foundation "Encours".

Open biography in PDF

1964

Sokolovskyi Serhii Ivanovych was born on April 16, 1964, in Dnipropetrovsk.

1982-1987

In 1987, he graduated from Dnipropetrovsk Medical Institute and received higher education in Dentistry. During his studies, he was awarded the "Inventor of the USSR" badge.

1987-2004

After graduating from the institute, he worked in medical institutions in Dnipropetrovsk, combining practical work with teaching at Dnipropetrovsk Medical Institute. Since 1992, he worked as a department head at the I.I. Mechnikov Regional Clinical Hospital.

2003

Parallel to his medical practice, he studied at the Dnipropetrovsk Regional Institute of State Administration of the National Academy of State Administration under the President of Ukraine, which he graduated in 2003 and received a Master's degree in Public Administration.

In the same year, he obtained a Master's degree in Humanities from London Metropolitan University (London, UK).

Also in 2003, he defended his thesis at the Odessa Institute of Dentistry of the Academy of Medical Sciences of Ukraine, where he was awarded the degree of Candidate of Medical Sciences.

2004-...

Since 2004 and to the present day, he has held the position of Vice-Rector for International Affairs at the European Medical University, and since 2008, he has been the Director of the International Research and Educational Center of Traditional Medicine at this University.

2005-2020

In 2005, he was invited to the Dnipropetrovsk Regional Institute of State Administration of the National Academy of State Administration under the President of Ukraine, where he worked as a senior researcher. After completing his doctoral studies, from 2008 to 2020, he worked as an Associate Professor of the Department of Public Administration and Local Self-Government.

2007

In 2007, he graduated from Dnipropetrovsk National University named after O. Gonchar, Faculty of Advanced Training in Medical Psychology, where he worked as an Associate Professor of the Department of Medical Psychology, Psychodiagnostics, and Psychotherapy on a part-time basis.

2007-2019

For two consecutive years in 2007-2008 and in 2014, he was awarded the Diploma of the Winner of the All-Ukrainian Competition "Invention 2007", "Invention 2008", "Invention 2014" in the nomination "Best Invention in Medicine and Pharmacology".

Awarded the Order "Scientist of the Year 2019", Honorary Diploma of the Ukrainian Federation of Scientists of Ukraine, Letter of Appreciation from the Ministry of Health of Ukraine, and Certificate of the Verkhovna Rada of Ukraine. For a scientific discovery in the field of biophysics, he was awarded the honorary medal named after P.L. Kapitsa.

Included in the book "Scientific Elite of Ukraine". Included in the fifth edition of "Pioneers of Modern Medicine" of the best medical workers of Ukraine. Author of more than 200 scientific and educational works, monographs, patents for invention, co-author of a scientific discovery in the field of biophysics.

Mykola Mykolaiovych Khmyl

First Deputy Director

Honorary Doctor of the International Academy of Cadres. Honorary Professor of the Association Internationale d’Ecologie et de Sante Health and Ecology International Association (Marseille, France).

Open biography in PDF

1960

Khmyl Mykola Mykolaiovych was born on January 4, 1960, in Chernihiv region, Ukraine.

1990

After graduating from Taras Shevchenko National University of Kyiv in 1990 with a degree in History, he worked as a specialist and referent.

2003

In 2003, he graduated from the National Academy of State Administration under the President of Ukraine in Public Administration and received a Master's degree in Public Administration.

2004

Since 2004, he has worked as an external consultant for the Special Control Commission of the Verkhovna Rada of Ukraine on privatization issues, and as an assistant-consultant to a member of parliament of Ukraine. State civil servant of 7th rank.

2005

In 2005, he defended his thesis at the Kharkiv National University of Internal Affairs, where he was awarded the degree of Candidate of Legal Sciences. Doctor of Philosophy in Law, Honorary Doctor of the International Academy of Cadres.

2007-2011

From 2007 to 2011, he worked as Deputy Director of the PMN branch of PJSC "Ukrtransnafta".

2009

In 2009, he graduated from the University of Economics and Law "KROK" with a degree in Financial and Economic Security Management at Ukrainian enterprises, combining education and work as head of the external relations department, chief engineer, department director, and coordination center director.

2011-2015

From 2011 to 2015 - President of the International Fund "Ukrainian Reforms".

2015-2016

From 2015 to 2016, he worked as Deputy Director of the Friendship branch, Head of the Security Department of PJSC "Ukrtransnafta", combining work with studies at the Ivano-Frankivsk National Technical University of Oil and Gas. Institute of Postgraduate Education, which he graduated from in 2016.

2020-...

Since 2020, he has been an expert and moderator of the Official Representation of the International Information Nobel Center in Ukraine.

2022-...

Since 2022 - First Deputy Director of the Official Representation of the International Information Nobel Center in Ukraine.
